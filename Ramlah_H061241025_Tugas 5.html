Bathymetric Morphometry & Basin Analysis

Key hydrographic findings from Danau Unhas survey

Central Deep Basin Concentration The maximum depths ($> 6.5\text{m}$) are heavily concentrated along the east-central section ($770600\text{E} - 770750\text{E}, 9436300\text{N}$). This acts as the primary retention basin for Unhas Lake.
Littoral Zone Expansion Shallow shorelines ($< 2.0\text{m}$) cover approximately **31.4%** of the total surface area. High slope gradients ($> 22^\circ$) near the Western inlet suggest ongoing bank instability.
Submerged Ridge Formation A distinct submerged ridge feature runs North-South at $770520\text{E}$, causing localized water stagnation and potential nutrient trapping.

Sedimentation & Mitigation Strategy

Actionable recommendations for UNHAS Estate Management

Targeted Maintenance Dredging Prioritize eco-friendly suction dredging along the Western Inlet Slope where sediment accumulation has reduced active storage by an estimated $14.2\%$.
Bio-engineering Slope Stabilization Construct vetiver grass bio-reinforcement barriers along embankment slopes exceeding $20^\circ$ to minimize runoff siltation from surrounding campus infrastructure.
Real-time Water Quality Monitoring Install automated telemetry buoys at the Deep Basin coordinates to monitor DO, turbidity, and thermal stratification year-round.
